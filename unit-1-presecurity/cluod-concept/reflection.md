# Cloud Computing Reflection

## 1. Cloud in your own words

For me, it means using computers and storage from another company through the internet instead of having everything on my own computer. Companies like Microsoft, Amazon, and Google have the computers, and we pay to use them. I think it is useful because I do not need to buy and take care of all the equipment myself.

## 2. Traditional → Cloud → Containers

I think the main idea is that computers and software have slowly become easier to use and manage. Before, companies needed their own physical computers, then they found ways to use one computer for several things, and later they started using computers owned by other companies through the internet. Containers are another step that makes it easier to move software from one computer to another.

## 3. Deployment vs Service models

The way I understand it, deployment models are about where the computer systems are, while service models are about what the company does for you. For example, with some services you have to manage more things yourself, while with others you just use the finished service. Microsoft 365 is a good example because I can use things like Outlook and Teams online without knowing where the actual computers are.

## 4. The Shared Responsibility Model

What I understood from this is that using a service from another company does not mean they are responsible for everything. They take care of their side, but I still have to take care of my account, password, settings, and the information I put there. 

## 5. Why organisations still hesitate

I can understand why some companies do not want to move everything online. They might have important or private information that they are not allowed to store just anywhere. Another reason could be money, because it can be hard to know exactly how much these services will cost when the amount of use changes.

## 6. Cloud in an entry-level tech role

I think I could come across these services even if my job was just helping people with their computers. For example, someone might tell me that they cannot log in to Microsoft acount or that their phone is not working for the login code. I might also have to help someone when a service they use online is not working, even if their own computer seems fine.

## 7. Your personal takeaway

My main takeaway is that I now understand that the cloud is not really something floating somewhere on the internet. There are real computers and people behind it, even though I do not see them. I also learned that small mistakes, like changing one wrong setting, can sometimes cause bigger problems than I would expect.
